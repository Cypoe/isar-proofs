ISAR — Machine, Quotient, and Proof Insights

The machine view and the formal view are the same story seen at different resolutions: IOB gives structure, SAR gives action, IRAS gives the invariant quotient, and the Lean proof work explains why “equal under normalization” is not a slogan but a typed object.

The core idea

ISAR matters because it does not start from a language, file format, or fixed rule table. It starts from existence, pairing, self-description, and asymmetry, then shows that any admissible surface must factor through an invariant layer if the system is closed under self-application. Banality vs closure

A fixed external rule table may still be universal, but it is banal as a foundation: rules are outside the carrier, so rule evolution requires a higher interpreter layer rather than happening within the same substrate. [file:97]

The four carriers

The proof-development summary identifies four irreducible roles: invariant identity, rewrite, adjacency, and state. Remove one and a whole family of equalities stops being expressible inside the same machine. [file:49][file:97]

Proof insight

The quotient is not a metaphor. The invariant layer is explicitly described as a quotient by operational equality, and unique normal forms are what make that quotient usable as a real target of computation. [file:97]
